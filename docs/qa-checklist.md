# ScoutBoard AI QA Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing screenshots, reports, or portfolio samples.

## Data

- Player and team required fields are complete.
- Numeric fields are plausible.
- Source URL, source name, and checked date are present.
- Synthetic records are clearly marked.
- Known flawed rows are excluded from final client-facing exports.

## Research

- Every factual claim has a source or is labeled as sample/synthetic.
- Scouting opinions are separated from facts.
- Dates and season context are visible.
- No copyrighted or restricted dataset is copied into the repo.

## Writing

- Draft wording is manually edited.
- No unsupported AI-generated claim remains.
- Client-facing language is concise and neutral.
- English/Japanese deliverables are checked by a human before use.

## App

- Dashboard loads without runtime errors.
- Player detail pages render for each sample player.
- Data QA page lists the intentionally flawed row.
- Unit tests pass.
- No secrets or personal files are staged.
